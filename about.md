# About

**Kyber ML** is a machine learning book built from first principles.

## Who This Is For

Engineers and researchers who want to understand ML at the level where maths, code, and intuition converge. If you've ever wondered *why* backpropagation works rather than just *how* to call `.fit()`, this book is for you.

## Who Writes It

**[RequieMa](https://github.com/RequieMa)** (魂麦) — engineer and author focused on statistical dynamics, complexity science, and cybernetics. The book grows out of hands-on work across computer vision, scientific computing, and AI systems.

This is a **living book** — chapters are published as they're written. What you see today is a
snapshot of an ongoing project. Corrections, feedback, and contributions are welcome.

## The Thesis

Everything ties to one idea: **complex adaptive systems can be understood through the lens of feedback, dynamics, and information.** 

Machine learning is the most visible instantiation of this principle — training a model *is* controlling a dynamical system toward lower loss. ML was born from statistics and basic maths of differentiation, yet the outcomes seems not to be explained easily in such reduction manner. We may need another way to see ML. This book hopes to make that connection explicit.
