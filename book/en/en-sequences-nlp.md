# Sequences & Natural Language Processing

> Aligns with tutorial/AI **M5 — Time Series & Sequences**.

Climb a ladder of harder sequences — forecasting, voice, language — meeting the RNN family. The LSTM is a gated RNN fix for forgetting, still sequential (slow) and still fades. By the top, even the LSTM struggles on language: exactly the struggle attention erases.

**Math thread:** recurrence and unrolling, BPTT → vanishing gradients, LSTM gates, the parallelization wall.
