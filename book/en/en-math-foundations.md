# Mathematical Foundations

> Aligns with tutorial/AI **M2 — The Math Engine, in numpy**.

Learning is one idea repeated: measure wrongness (loss), step downhill (gradient descent). This chapter builds the engine by hand — vectors and matrices, derivatives and gradients, the update rule $w \leftarrow w - \eta \nabla L$.

**Math thread:** dot products, matrix multiplication, the gradient as "downhill direction," MSE, learning rate dynamics.
